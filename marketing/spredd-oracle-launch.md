## How it works

The loop is simple and takes about 30 seconds a day:

1. **Predict every match.** Before kickoff, call each match — home, away, or draw. Lock it in.
2. **Earn points.** Correct picks score points, settled automatically off live scores.
3. **Don't break your streak.** String together winning days and your multiplier climbs — up to **2x**. Miss a day, and it resets to zero. Show up.

That's the daily rhythm. But two things make Oracle more than a pick'em.

### The bracket

Before the tournament kicks off, fill out your bracket: pick the two teams advancing from each group, your group winners, and — the big one — **your champion**. Nail the champion you called weeks earlier and it's worth **+200 points**. It's the single most valuable call in the game, and it locks at the first whistle on June 11.

## The rewards

We've committed **$1,000 in $SPRDD** to the prize pool across the tournament:

- **Daily reward pools** on every match day, split among that day's top scorers, pro-rata by points.
- **A $200 final jackpot** reserved for the players who called the champion correctly, plus the top of the overall leaderboard.
- Pools are **weighted toward the knockouts**, when every match is win-or-go-home.

Every reward is paid in $SPRDD **on-chain**. Rewards vest over a week and you claim them straight to your connected wallet on Base — no forms, no waiting on a payout email.

And here's the part that closes the loop: the reward pool is **bought, not printed**. Every token paid out is acquired through a daily buyback. So the more the game is played, the more $SPRDD gets bought off the market. Engagement and the token pull in the same direction.
